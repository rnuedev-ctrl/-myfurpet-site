Is MyFurPet completely free for pet owners? +
Yes! All core pet owner features — including digital medical passports, vaccination countdowns, AI scanner, partner clinic booking passes, daily care checklists, and PawAlert SOS alerts — are 100% free.
How do I install the Android APK or iOS TestFlight build? +
On Android, you can download the direct APK file from our releases or join our Google Play internal tester track. On iOS, sign up with your Apple ID email above to receive an instant Apple TestFlight invitation link.
How does the AI Vet Record Scanner protect my pet's data? +
All scanned medical records are encrypted and tied strictly to your private account and pet ID. We do not sell your personal data or clinic records to third parties.
I own a Vet Clinic or Pet Grooming business. How do I join as a Partner? +
You can register your clinic directly inside the app under Profile ➔ 'Are You a Vet Clinic or Pet Business?', or contact us via our partner portal to get verified, receive booking leads, and manage patient digital check-ins.
