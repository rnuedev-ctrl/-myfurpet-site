Account & Personal Data Erasure

You retain full ownership and sovereign control over your personal data. Learn how to permanently delete your MyFurPet account, medical cards, and cloud backups instantly.

📱 Method 1: Instant In-App Account Deletion (Recommended)

For maximum security and immediate real-time erasure, you can delete your account directly within the MyFurPet mobile app on iOS or Android:

01

Open Profile Tab

Launch the MyFurPet mobile application and tap the Profile 👤 tab in the bottom navigation bar.

02

Access Settings

Scroll down to the App Preferences & Account ⚙️ section located under your pet listings.

03

Tap Delete Account

Tap the red button labeled 🗑️ Delete Account & Personal Data to open the security modal.

04

Type "DELETE" to Confirm

Type the uppercase word DELETE and confirm. All cloud records and authentication tokens are wiped immediately.

⚠️ What Is Permanently Purged Upon Deletion:
  • User Profile: Name, verified email address, authentication credentials, and avatars.
  • Pet Health Records: All registered pet profiles, vaccination cards, medical photos, and clinic histories.
  • Booking Passes & GPS Logs: Vet appointment vouchers, chat threads, and GPS walk route polylines.
  • Wallets & Tokens: AI visual scan tokens and PawPoints rewards wallet.
