Privacy Policy for MyFurPet

Last updated: August 2026

Welcome to MyFurPet. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your information is collected, used, and protected.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us when using the application, including:

  • Account details (name, email address, profile photo).
  • Pet profiles (pet name, breed, age, medical records, vaccination dates).
  • Appointment & booking requests with verified clinics.
  • Photos uploaded for pet profiles or clinic records.

2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

To support free services, MyFurPet displays ads served by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may use device identifiers and cookies to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements in compliance with applicable laws.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to provide, maintain, and improve app functionality, such as managing pet health records, facilitating appointments, and powering in-app features.

4. Data Security

All data is securely transmitted and stored using industry-standard encryption protocols.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or privacy requests regarding MyFurPet, please contact our support team.
