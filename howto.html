How To Use MyFurPet 📖

Easy, step-by-step beginner walkthroughs for pet parents, verified veterinary clinics, groomers, and pet supply merchants.

🔍
🚀 Getting Started

🐾 Adding Your Pet & Digital Profile

🌟 WHY THIS MATTERS Creates a cloud-synced digital identity with their breed, age, allergies, and microchip number accessible from any device.
📍 Where to find it: Home 🏠 ➔ Add Pet Button (+) ➔ Fill Info ➔ Save Pet
  • 1Tap the "+" button or "Add Pet" card on the Home screen.
  • 2Enter your pet's name, species (Dog, Cat, Rabbit, etc.), breed, gender, and birth date.
  • 3Upload their cutest photo. Tap "Save Pet" to generate their digital passport!
💡 Beginner Pro Tip: Enter their microchip or NFC ID so finders can instantly identify them if lost.
🩺 Health & Passport

💉 AI Vaccine Card Scanner & Passport

🌟 WHY THIS MATTERS Paper clinic booklets easily get wet, torn, or misplaced. Digitizing immunizations ensures hassle-free vet visits and travel approval.
📍 Where to find it: Home 🏠 ➔ Tap Pet Card ➔ Medical Passport ➔ Scan Card
  • 1Tap your pet's card from Home to open their Pet Details.
  • 2Navigate to the "Vaccines" tab, then tap "📸 AI Scan Clinic Card".
  • 3Take a clear photo of the handwritten clinic booklet. AI automatically parses vaccine names and due dates!
💡 Beginner Pro Tip: Tap "Export PDF Passport" to generate an official digital certificate for borders, hotels, and airlines.
🚨 Emergency & Lost Pet

🚨 PawAlert 5km Lost Pet Amber Alert

🌟 WHY THIS MATTERS The first 3 hours after a pet escapes are the most critical. Instantly broadcasting an alert to nearby pet owners increases recovery by 80%.
📍 Where to find it: Menu ☰ ➔ PawAlert (Lost Pet & SOS) ➔ Broadcast Alert
  • 1Tap "PawAlert (Lost Pet & SOS)" in the Home menu or the red SOS button.
  • 2Confirm your pet's last seen GPS location, photo, and emergency phone number.
  • 3An instant push notification is broadcast to all MyFurPet users within a 5km radius!
💡 Beginner Pro Tip: The app automatically generates a printable Lost Pet Flyer with your contact QR code and reward info.
🪙 PawPoints Vault

👑 PawPoints™ Vault & Royal Crown Flair

🌟 WHY THIS MATTERS Gamifies healthy habits! Every walk, habit check, and review earns points redeemable for VIP passes, AI tokens, and animated avatar frames.
📍 Where to find it: Header [🪙 pts] Pill ➔ Rewards Vault ➔ "Flair" Tab
  • 1Tap the [🪙 pts] pill in the top header (or via Menu ➔ PawPoints Rewards).
  • 2Redeem the Royal Crown Gold Ring 👑 (200 pts) in the Vault tab.
  • 3Switch to the "Flair" tab and tap "Equip" to have your pet wear the floating 24k crown and golden aura!
💡 Beginner Pro Tip: Complete a 7-day care streak to unlock a 2x bonus points multiplier!
🩺 Health & Passport

💊 Smart Pillbox & Refill Alarms

🌟 WHY THIS MATTERS Missing antibiotics or heartworm doses causes drug resistance and health setbacks. Smart pillbox alarms keep dosages on time.
📍 Where to find it: Home 🏠 ➔ Tap Pet Card ➔ Medications Tab ➔ Add Pill
  • 1Open your pet's "Medications" tab in their details profile.
  • 2Enter the medication name, dose, frequency, and treatment duration.
  • 3Receive high-priority alarms with 1-tap "Mark as Given" tracking.
💡 Beginner Pro Tip: Enable "Refill Warning" to get alerted 3 days before pills run out so you can reorder smoothly.
🚶 Activity & Walking

🐕 GPS Walk Tracker & Paw Heat Safety

🌟 WHY THIS MATTERS Hot asphalt burns sensitive paw pads in just 60 seconds. Tracking routes and weather keeps your walks safe and energizing.
📍 Where to find it: Home 🏠 ➔ Weather Widget / Menu ➔ GPS Walk Tracker
  • 1Check the "Paw Pad Thermal Risk" gauge on Home before leashing up.
  • 2Open the GPS Walk Tracker and tap "Start Walk" to record live distance, pace, and calories.
  • 3Tap "Finish Walk" to save the route to your activity log and claim +15 PawPoints!
💡 Beginner Pro Tip: If the gauge shows "High Heat", test pavement with the back of your hand for 7 seconds first!
👨‍👩‍👧 Family & Sharing

👨‍👩‍👧 Co-Parenting & Pet Sitter Sharing

🌟 WHY THIS MATTERS Allows partners, family members, or pet sitters to log walks, medications, and meals in real time so vital care is never duplicated.
📍 Where to find it: Home 🏠 ➔ Co-Parenting Banner / Menu ➔ Invite Caretaker
  • 1Open "Family & Sitter Co-Parenting" from the Home menu or pet options.
  • 2Enter the email of your partner, family member, or pet sitter.
  • 3Select their permission tier (Full Co-Parent or Daily Caretaker) and send the invite code.
💡 Beginner Pro Tip: Sitters can record meals and meds without accessing your payment methods or billing info.
🎓 Training & Behavior

🎓 Pet Training Planner & Lessons

🌟 WHY THIS MATTERS Structured positive reinforcement training prevents destructive chewing, eases separation anxiety, and ensures reliable leash recall.
📍 Where to find it: Home 🏠 ➔ Pet Training Planner Card ➔ Browse Lessons
  • 1Scroll to the "Pet Training Planner" card on Home or open it from pet details.
  • 2Choose beginner tricks (Sit, Stay, Come) or behavioral routines (Crate, Loose Leash).
  • 3Follow timer drills, log completed sessions, and earn bonus PawPoints!
💡 Beginner Pro Tip: 5-minute daily sessions yield much better recall than one long weekly session.
🏥 Vet Practice Setup

🏥 Clinic Registration & PRC Verification

🌟 WHY THIS MATTERS Places your practice on the map for thousands of pet owners and validates your professional credentials with a verified clinic badge.
📍 Where to find it: Profile 👤 ➔ Register Vet / Partner ➔ Fill Clinic Info
  • 1In Profile 👤, tap "Register as Vet / Business Partner".
  • 2Select "Veterinary Clinic", enter your clinic name, PRC license number, and operating hours.
  • 3Indicate whether your hospital provides 24/7 Emergency & Critical ICU care.
💡 Beginner Pro Tip: Verified practices receive prominent priority placement on the Places map directory.
🏥 Vet Practice Setup

🩻 Diagnostic Equipment Badges

🌟 WHY THIS MATTERS Pet owners search specifically for clinics with in-house diagnostics. Highlighting your tools directs qualified patients to your practice.
📍 Where to find it: Partner Portal ➔ Edit Clinic ➔ Select Equipment
  • 1Check off in-house equipment: Digital X-Ray, Blood Chemistry Lab, Ultrasound, ICU.
  • 2Highlight clinical specialties: Soft Tissue Surgery, Orthopedics, Feline Medicine, Dentistry.
  • 3Save changes to display badges prominently in search filters.
💡 Beginner Pro Tip: Clinics with Digital X-Ray and Ultrasound badges receive 3x more emergency surgical inquiries.
✂️ Grooming Setup

✂️ Salon Setup & Rabies Passport Checks

🌟 WHY THIS MATTERS Allows pet parents to discover your salon, inspect haircut portfolios, and guarantees all booked pets have verified rabies vaccinations.

Beginner FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add multiple pets to one account?

Yes! You can add unlimited dogs, cats, rabbits, and birds. Switch between them on the Home screen in 1 tap.

Is MyFurPet free to use for pet parents?

Yes! Tracking vaccines, smart pillbox alarms, weight logs, QR collar tags, and clinic bookings are 100% free.

How do I share my pet's medical passport with my vet?

Tap your pet's card on Home, go to Vaccines, and tap "Export PDF Passport". You can email the PDF or show the verified QR pass at the clinic.

Do my earned PawPoints ever expire?

No, earned PawPoints never expire! Accumulate points via daily care habits and redeem them anytime in the Rewards Vault.
