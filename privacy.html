MyFurPet Privacy Policy

📅 Last Updated: August 2026 • Compliant with Republic Act No. 10173 & Global Standards
MyFurPet ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of pet owners, pet patients, and veterinary partners. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, secure, and manage your data when using the MyFurPet mobile application and web ecosystem.

⚖️ 1. Compliance with Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)

In strict compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012 of the Philippines) and international data protection standards, we ensure that all personal information and sensitive veterinary records collected through our platform are processed with transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality.

📋 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide top-tier pet health tracking and veterinary booking services:

  • User Account Profile: Name, verified email address, phone number, location, and authentication credentials.
  • Pet Medical & Health Records: Pet name, breed, species, date of birth, weight history, vaccine certificates, scanned clinic notes, and medication schedules.
  • GPS & Location Data: Real-time location coordinates when tracking dog walks, finding nearby veterinary clinics, or broadcasting emergency Lost Pet SOS alerts.
  • OCR Scanned Documents: Medical receipts, clinic invoices, and vaccination cards processed privately via our AI record scanner.

🔒 3. How We Use and Protect Your Data

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-grade TLS 1.3 and Cloud Firestore security rules. We use your information strictly to:

  • Digitize and maintain your pet's portable digital health passport.
  • Facilitate instant appointment bookings with verified partner vet clinics.
  • Calculate pet health analytics, daily walk streak tracking, and calorie benchmarks.
  • Broadcast instant Lost Pet PawAlert SOS radar notifications to community members in your area.

🤝 4. Third-Party Processors & Zero Data Selling

🚫 Zero Data Selling Guarantee:

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data or your pet's medical records to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

We work exclusively with certified infrastructure partners to power app features:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase: Secure enterprise database storage, asset hosting, and user authentication.
  • Google Maps Services: Real-time geolocation resolution for veterinary clinic routing and dog walk tracking.
  • RevenueCat: Encrypted in-app subscription and VIP entitlement management.

🗑️ 5. Data Retention & Permanent Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may export or permanently delete your account, pet profiles, and medical records at any time.

To request permanent account and data erasure, tap "Delete Account" directly inside the app settings, or visit our online Account Deletion Page. All records will be permanently purged immediately.
