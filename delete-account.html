🐾 MyFurPet Privacy Policy

🗑️ Request Account & Data Deletion

At MyFurPet, we respect your right to privacy and full control over your personal data. You can delete your account and all associated personal data instantly at any time directly inside the app or through this page.

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the MyFurPet mobile app on iOS or Android.
  2. Tap on your Profile tab in the bottom navigation.
  3. Scroll to the Account & Settings section.
  4. Tap 🗑️ Delete Account & Personal Data.
  5. Confirm the permanent deletion prompt. Your account and all cloud data will be purged immediately.

Option 2: Online Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the app, you can submit an account removal request by contacting our data protection team at rnuedev@gmail.com with your registered email address. All records will be permanently erased within 24 hours.

Data Purged Upon Deletion:

  • User profile, name, email, authentication tokens, and avatars.
  • All registered pet profiles, medical cards, and vaccination photos.
  • Appointment booking records, clinic chat histories, and GPS walk polylines.
  • AI token balance and PawPoints reward wallet.
