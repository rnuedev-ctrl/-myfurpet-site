🚀 Getting Started 🐶 Pet Parents

🐾 Adding Your First Pet & Digital Profile

🌟 WHY THIS MATTERS Creates a cloud-synced digital identity for your pet with their breed, age, allergies, and microchip number accessible from any device.
📍 Where to find it: Home 🏠 ➔ Add Pet Button (+) ➔ Fill Info ➔ Save

  • 2 Enter your pet's name, species (Dog, Cat, Rabbit, etc.), breed, gender, and birth date.

🌟 WHY THIS MATTERS Resting respiratory rate is the #1 early warning sign of congestive heart failure. Knowing your pet's baseline saves their life in an emergency.
📍 Where to find it: Home 🏠 ➔ Tap Pet Card ➔ Health Passport ➔ Vitals Log
  • 1 In your pet's Medical Passport, tap "Vitals & Physiological Metrics".
  • 2 Measure Resting Respiratory Rate (RRR) while your pet is asleep (count breaths for 30 seconds and multiply by 2).
  • 3 Log temperature, heart rate, or blood glucose levels and compare them against normal breed ranges.
💡 Beginner Pro Tip: Sleeping respiratory rates exceeding 30 breaths per minute require immediate veterinary evaluation.

Answers by Role

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from pet parents, veterinary doctors, groomers, and merchants.

🐶 Pet Parents

Can I add multiple pets to my account?

Yes! You can add as many pets as you want (dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, etc.). Switch between them on the Home screen by tapping their avatar cards.

🐶 Pet Parents

Is PawsAI a substitute for a live veterinarian?

No. PawsAI is an intelligent triage and symptom checker designed to help you make informed decisions and prepare for clinic visits. In acute emergencies, trauma, or toxic ingestion, always take your pet to a licensed 24/7 veterinary hospital immediately.

🐶 Pet Parents

Do my PawPoints expire?

No, your earned PawPoints never expire! You can accumulate points by completing daily care habits, GPS walks, and reviews, then redeem them whenever you want in the Rewards Vault.

🐶 Pet Parents

How do I share my pet’s medical records with my vet?

Tap your pet’s card on Home, go to the "Vaccines" tab, and tap "Export PDF Passport". You can send the digital PDF directly to your vet via chat or email, or show the verified QR clinic pass at the front desk.

🐶 Pet Parents

How does PawAlert (Lost Pet & Emergency SOS) work?

When you broadcast an alert from the menu or SOS button, an instant Amber Alert push notification is sent to all registered MyFurPet owners within a 5km radius, and a high-visibility lost flyer with your contact QR is pinned to the Community Feed.

🐶 Pet Parents

Can my partner or pet sitter access our pet without my password?

Yes! Use "Family Co-Parenting & Sitter Sharing" from the Home menu or pet options to invite them via email. They get shared access to log daily care, walks, and medications securely.

🏥 Vets & Clinics

How long does clinic verification take?

Clinic registrations with valid PRC license numbers are typically reviewed and verified within 24 to 48 hours, unlocking the verified badge and appointment management tools.

🏥 Vets & Clinics

Can pet owners see my personal contact details?

No. Pet owners only see your published clinic business phone, address, operating hours, and in-app chat thread. Your personal account information remains completely private.

🏥 Vets & Clinics

How do Lead Credits work, and do they expire?

Every partner receives 15 FREE Lead Credits automatically on the 1st of every month. Credits are only deducted when an appointment is successfully completed. If a client cancels or no-shows, you can mark it as a No-Show to dispute and retain your credit. Purchased top-up credits never expire and roll over indefinitely.

✂️ Groomers

Can I require proof of rabies vaccination before booking?

Yes! The booking system allows you to require an active rabies vaccination record on the pet’s digital passport before confirming any grooming appointment.

🛍️ Pet Stores

How do deals appear on the Homepage Deals Strip?

Active promotions created through your merchant portal are automatically featured on the Homepage Pet Deals Strip, visible to pet parents in your geographic radius.
