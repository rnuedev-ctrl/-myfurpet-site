MyFurPet Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

MyFurPet ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Compliance with Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)

In compliance with Republic Act No. 10173, also known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 of the Philippines, we ensure that all personal and sensitive information collected through our application is processed with confidentiality, integrity, and adherence to data privacy principles.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when creating an account, registering as a partner clinic, uploading pet records, or tracking walks:

  • Account Data: Name, email address, phone number, physical location, and password credentials.
  • Pet Information: Pet name, species, age, vaccine records, medical photos, and history.
  • Location & GPS Data: Real-time location coordinates to track pet walks via GPS and display nearby places (vets, grooming, parks).
  • Financial Records: Billing receipts scanned via OCR for expense tracking, stored privately.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with third-party processors necessary for app functionality:

  • Firebase/Google: For database management, asset storage, and authentication.
  • Google Maps API: To fetch geolocation data for the places directory and walk tracking.
  • RevenueCat: To process VIP subscription packages and entitlements securely.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): To serve ad impressions to free tier accounts.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain personal information as long as your account remains active. You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated pet, location, and billing history at any time by contacting our support team at rnuedev@gmail.com.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at rnuedev@gmail.com.
